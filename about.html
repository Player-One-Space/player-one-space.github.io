About Player One Space

Building the roads and power lines of space — the essential infrastructure that everything else runs on.

The Problem

Space is becoming critical infrastructure — but the systems that support it haven't kept up.

🌎

Disconnected Earth

~90% of the planet lacks reliable connectivity for remote assets — pipelines, ships, mines, and farms remain offline and invisible.

🛰

Blind Satellites

Thousands of satellites operate with limited health monitoring, minimal data infrastructure, and aging propulsion technology.

⚡

Growing Urgency

As humanity puts more assets in space and depends on more on the ground, scalable, secure, and efficient space infrastructure is no longer optional.

Player One Space starts with connectivity and monitoring, then expands into the full orbital infrastructure stack: storage, compute, propulsion, and beyond.

What We Do

Our work spans three interconnected areas, all built on a shared orbital backbone.

🛰

Satellite IoT Network

Our Core — Connectivity Where None Exists

A LEO constellation of small satellites that act like cell towers in the sky. Ground sensors on remote assets send small data packets up to our satellites, which relay them to our software platform. Customers see real-time dashboards, alerts, and reports — for assets that were previously invisible.

🛡

Earth & Space Monitoring

Eyes on What Matters

Monitoring solutions for both remote terrestrial assets and satellites in orbit. We provide real-time telemetry, health tracking, and analytics — helping operators maintain situational awareness whether the asset is on the ground, at sea, or in space.

🔬

R&D & Disruptive Technologies

Investing in the Future of Space

We actively research and develop next-generation space technologies — including orbital compute and storage, advanced propulsion systems, and defense applications. Our R&D portfolio explores what's possible when space infrastructure becomes programmable.
