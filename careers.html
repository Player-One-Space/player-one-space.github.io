Help us build the next great space company — out of Canada, for the world.

Player One Space is a Canadian space technology company headquartered in Vancouver, BC, building at the intersection of Space, AI, and Data. Our team has shipped real spacecraft hardware, operated LEO constellations, and built large-scale AI/data platforms at Amazon, Microsoft, and Fortune 500s. We are now building the satellites, ground systems, and software platform that will define the next decade of commercial space.

If you want to build hardware on orbit and intelligence on orbit, and do it from the ground floor of a generational company, we want to hear from you.
